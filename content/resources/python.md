## Programming in Python

Offered in **Data Science (Foundation)** and **Electronic Systems (Diploma)**.

### Data Science — Foundation

- **Course ID:** CS1002
- **Course Credits:** 4
- **Course Type:** Foundational
- **Pre-requisites:** BSCS1001 — Computational Thinking

### Electronic Systems — Diploma

- **Course ID:** CS2102
- **Course Credits:** 4

- **Pre-requisites:** None
